# Nabrio 0.4.0

**POV Rajput is now Nabrio.** Same app, new name and logo. This is a normal update: your settings, logins, add-ons, debrid services, Usenet providers, watch history and cloud backup all stay as they are.

## New name, new look

- **Nabrio** (from news**nab** + de**brid**): the app's name everywhere, in the launcher, Settings and the update screen.
- **New logo:** a red and white stream ring around a play button, on black. The logo inside the app is now round, on the opening screen and everywhere else.
- **New website address: [nabrio.rajput.cc](https://nabrio.rajput.cc).** The old pov.rajput.cc keeps working and takes you there.
- Your **POV account** is now your **Nabrio account**: same account, same password, same backups.

## New

- **Watched ✓ on episode thumbnails** in the episode row on a show's page and in the player's Episodes panel, like the episode list already had.
- **Easynews NNTP pipeline** (Settings → Sources → Easynews → NNTP pipeline): how many pieces each connection asks for at once, like the news providers' setting. **Default 2, up to 20** (it was always 1 before), which streams faster on far-away servers.

## Better

- **Smoother side menu.** It now slides and fades away in one piece instead of shrinking with a jerk.
- **The seek bar's time bubble shows just the time** (1:49:14), so it no longer covers the bar and the subtitles.

## Fixed

- **Links from other shows on short titles.** For a show like *Dark*, some add-ons also returned episodes of *Dark Angel*, *Darkness*, *Web of Darkness* and *The Dark*. Episode links whose names clearly belong to another show are now hidden, and Easynews and Usenet searches no longer match "The Dark" for "Dark". Links named in other languages are kept, since they are often right.
