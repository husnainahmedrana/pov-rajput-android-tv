# POV Rajput 0.3.17

## New

- **Cast.** Title pages show the cast with their photos. Select an actor to see their movies and shows; See all lists every title, newest first.
- **Badge styles.** Settings → Playback → Links & sorting → Badge style: Color by type (default), Aurora, Sunset or Elite. Audio tags (DDP, Atmos, DTS-HD MA, TrueHD, 5.1…) are now shown, and related tags are grouped.
- **Compact link list.** About twice as many links fit on screen: one-line names (the selected link shows its full name) and the source at the end of the badges. It can be switched off.
- **Link sorting your way.** Move Language, Quality and File size up or down and set each one's direction, with Reset to default.
- **AIOStreams.** Its links show the AIOStreams logo with the debrid service or your Usenet news provider (choose it per add-on under Source providers → Manage) and the indexer. Links already in your library come first with an In library badge. Your AIOStreams library appears in the Library tab.
- **Search and details from your own add-on.** Settings → Home & appearance → Search and details: use a catalog add-on such as a self-hosted AIOMetadata for faster, typo-tolerant search and richer title details. Cinemeta stays the fallback.
- **TVDB for shows.** Add your own TVDB key for fuller season and episode lists.
- **SubDL subtitles.** Add your free SubDL key next to Wyzie for more subtitle languages.
- **PlavioHD** links show their logo.

## Changed

- Playback settings are split into Links & sorting, Auto-play and Player pages.
- The Subtitles tab is now Audio & Subtitles, with separate English fallbacks for audio and subtitles and a default volume boost.
- Back from Settings or any menu page lands on Home's rows, not the menu.

## Fixed

- With a subtitle language other than English, subtitles in that language are now searched online even when the video has English built in.
- On videos you can't seek (live streams), Left and Right now move past Pause to the other player buttons.
- AIOStreams Usenet links wait longer and retry quietly after a seek instead of failing.
