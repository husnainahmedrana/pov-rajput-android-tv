# POV Rajput 0.3.18

## New

- **Trailers** are available now (TV). Settings → Home & appearance → Trailers.
- **Continue Watching on your TV's home screen.** POV can add its own Continue Watching row and Play Next cards to Android TV launchers. Your launcher decides whether and where the row appears. Switch it off under Settings → Home & appearance → TV home Continue Watching.
