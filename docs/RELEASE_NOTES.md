# POV Rajput 0.3.11

## New

- **One-press speed.** The player's **Speed** button now steps through **1× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 1.75× → 2× → 1×** with each press, and the new speed flashes briefly on screen. Hold OK on Speed (long-press on the phone) for the full list, including 0.5× and 0.75×.

## Changes

- **Phone show pages.** On the phone, show and anime pages are back to a vertical episode list: still, number, air date, title and a two-line synopsis, with the season tabs above it. The TV keeps the sideways episode row.
- **Player panels.** Streams, Audio & Subtitles, Speed and the other player panels use the softer fade again. Hold-OK menus keep the quicker one.
