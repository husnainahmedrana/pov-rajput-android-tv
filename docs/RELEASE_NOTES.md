# POV Rajput 0.3.25

## New

- **Link filters as tiles.** Qualities to show, Hide and Prefer are now one page of tiles: each has a name, a short hint and a switch, and a switched-off tile is dimmed and crossed out, so you see at a glance what is on. Your own words, release groups and patterns are rows below the tiles.
- **Separate Prefer switches for Dolby Vision, HDR, Atmos and lossless audio.** Each one only counts when your device can use it: Dolby Vision needs a Dolby Vision decoder, HDR needs 10-bit HEVC, and Atmos needs audio passthrough. A switch your device can't use says so on its tile and is ignored. If you had the old combined switches on, both new ones are on.
- **Links under your auto-play limit come first.** With auto-play and its limit on, links over the limit move below an "Over your auto-play limit" heading. Both groups keep your Link sorting order, so the link auto-play starts is at the top. The player's Streams panel uses the same order.
- **Tiles for Hide release groups, Subtitle style and Trailers.** Release groups to hide are tiles you switch on or off (hold OK to remove one). Subtitle style has preset tiles with a sample of each look, fonts each shown in their own font, color swatches, outline and box choices, and size tiles. Trailers' four switches sit in one row.
- **A cleaner About page.** The app and its version on top, then Report a bug, Request a feature, Website and Open-source notices, then the services POV uses as a grid of logos.

## Improved

- Samples and trailers are hidden by default. If you changed that switch yourself, your choice is kept.
- File size now sorts largest first by default. A saved choice is kept.
- The auto-play bitrate limit (1–500 Mbps) and the movie and episode size fallbacks (1–1000 GB) use the Up/Down picker and move by one per press, so any number can be chosen. Hold the button to go faster.
- The player's Audio, Subtitles and other panels are see-through like the Streams panel.
- The buttons in the Link sorting window line up on every row.
- The small credit line at the bottom of Home is gone; credits stay in Settings → About → Open-source notices.
