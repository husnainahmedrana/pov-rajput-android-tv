# POV Rajput 0.3.24

## New

- **Links appear as they arrive.** All add-ons are searched at the same time and links show up as each one answers, with a "Searching 5/9 · waiting for …" line. Any number of source providers can be added now.
- **Search goal.** Choose when a search is done: when the max search time ends, once a number of good links are in (1–40), once Best per quality is full, or whichever of those two comes first. Good links play at once, suit your device and fit your Auto-play limit.
- **Max search time.** One time for all add-ons, and any add-on can have its own, shorter or longer. Settings → Source providers shows each add-on's average answer time.
- **Best per quality.** Show only the best few links of each quality (4K, 1080p, 720p, lower) in your sort order, with a More links row for the rest. **Qualities to show** switches whole qualities on or off.
- **Link rules.** Prefer or hide links by keywords, release groups, ready-made rules and regex patterns, like AIOStreams SEL templates.
  - **Prefer:** your own keywords (each in its own field, first ranks highest), ready-made rules (Remux/Blu-ray, WEB-DL over WEBRip, HEVC, 10-bit, HDR/Dolby Vision, Atmos/lossless, Proper/Repack) and trusted release groups, each list and each group switchable. A new movable **Preferred** row in Link sorting decides where they rank.
  - **Hide:** bad copies, 3D, upscales, hardcoded subs, samples, dubbed audio, Remux, TV recordings, AV1, x264, Dolby Vision only links your device can't play, links naming a different year (another film with the same name), low-quality release groups, your own words and a pattern. The link list can still show what was hidden.
- **Auto-play modes.** Off, first good link, best link after a few seconds, or best link when the search ends, with a 3-second "Playing … " countdown you can stop with any key. A show's next episode prefers the release group you watched last. If none of the top links fit your Auto-play limit, the best fitting link from all links is used.
- **Filter links by source.** On the link list, go Up to the service and add-on logos: landing on one shows only its links, Back shows all again.
- **New Streams panel in the player.** A see-through panel over the video, a column of add-ons on the left to filter by, and the link now playing pinned on top.
- **Crew on details pages.** Directors, writers, composers and more, each opening the films they worked on.
- **Subtitle style.** Fonts (Lato by default, plus Roboto, Arimo, Inter, Open Sans, Noto Sans and more), text and outline colors, outline type (outline, drop shadow, raised, depressed), a background box with see-through levels, presets and a live preview. Fonts and presets are also in the player's Subtitle style menu.

## Improved

- Continue Watching keeps a title's last link for 7 days (was 1) and up to 60 titles; a link that no longer works gives way to a fresh search faster.
- Show pages download their cast and crew list once, and crew photos only load when that row comes into view.
- Details pages open on Play.
- Up/Down number pickers for times, counts and Easynews connections.
- Long link lists build in steps, so the TV stays responsive, and the link list no longer works behind the player while a video plays.
- The new link and search settings are part of your account backup.
