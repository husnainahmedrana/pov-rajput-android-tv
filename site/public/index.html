Your streams, on the big screen.

POV Rajput is a free, open-source app for Android TV and phones. Stream from your debrid cloud, Easynews and Stremio add-ons in a cinematic interface, with WeTrakr or Simkl tracking and a fast player built for the remote.

⬇ Android TV POV-Rajput-TV.apk ⬇ Phone POV-Rajput-Mobile.apk
Latest version from GitHub. Android 8.0 or later.

Everything in one app

No Kodi, no ads, no tracking. Every key you enter stays encrypted on your device.

Flix interface

A living backdrop that follows your focus, title logos, a rotating feature and numbered Top 10 shelves. Smooth even on modest TV boxes.

Debrid and Easynews

Offcloud, TorBox, Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize and Debrid-Link, with your own order and on/off switches. Easynews over parallel connections.

Your add-ons

Add Stremio stream add-ons and catalog add-ons such as AIOMetadata, and arrange every Home row the way you like.

Play that just works

Play checks every source and your debrid caches at once, starts the best link and resumes where you left off.

A player for the remote

Precise seeking, seek previews, skip intro, recap and credits, Next Episode, a Streams panel to switch links, and Dolby and DTS audio.

WeTrakr or Simkl

Continue Watching, watchlists, Up Next and scrobbling with your own tracking account.

Encrypted backup

A free POV account backs up your whole setup. Sign in on any TV or phone and it all comes back.

Open source

GPL-3.0. Every line of the app and its backup server is on GitHub.

Made for the TV

Captured on a 55" TV in the app's public preview mode, which shows public catalogs only.

Install

POV Rajput isn't on the Play Store, so you install it yourself. It takes a couple of minutes.

Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV

  1. Install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews) from your TV's app store.
  2. Open Downloader and type pov.rajput.cc/tv, then press Go.
  3. When Android asks, allow Downloader to install unknown apps, then press Install.
  4. Open POV Rajput. Connect your debrid services and add-ons in Settings, or sign in to your POV account to bring your setup back.

Phone

  1. On your phone, open pov.rajput.cc/phone to download the app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Allow your browser to install unknown apps if asked.
  3. Press Install and open POV Rajput.

Updates: Settings → Updates → Check for updates installs new versions and keeps your accounts and settings.

Your account, only yours

Create a free POV account in Settings → Account and your setup is backed up whenever you leave the app. Debrid keys, add-ons and logins are encrypted on your device with your password before they are sent, so the server can't open them.

WhatWho can open it
Debrid services, add-ons, TMDB / Easynews / Seekr / Wyzie keys, WeTrakr and Simkl loginsOnly you, with your password or recovery key
Home rows, preferences, local watch progressEncrypted with a key the server keeps, so a password reset by email can bring them back
Your email addressKept only to send sign-up and password-reset codes
Your password and recovery keyNever sent anywhere

Forgot your password? Reset it with an email code. With your recovery key everything comes back; without it, your settings do. Read the privacy policy or the backup server's source.
